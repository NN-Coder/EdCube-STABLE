# Legal Policy Generator Skill

## Overview
Assists in drafting and maintaining site-critical legal documentation including DMCA, Terms of Service, and Privacy Policy.

## Capabilities
- Generate standard templates for DMCA Takedown notices.
- Draft ToS clauses regarding user-generated content (chat, comments).
- Update Privacy Policy to reflect data storage in Supabase and R2.

## Rules
- INCLUDE mandatory contact information for the registered agent.
- ENSURE all policies are accessible via the site footer.
- USE clear, non-legalese language where possible, while maintaining legal validity.
- REFERENCE the specific infrastructure used (Supabase, Cloudflare) in data disclosure sections.
